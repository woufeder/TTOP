# The Ash Road

After the northern firefall, only blackened milestones and broken military roads remained across the snowfield. Ariane carried a fragment of a star map while riding beside a border company through watchposts that no longer had names.

She knew the journey was not only about recovering a map. If the old prophecy was true, one of the roads erased from imperial records would reveal itself again when the night sky turned.

Soren drew the reins tight and looked toward a pass buried under grey snow.

"Beyond that point begins the land that was never written into any archive."

Ariane slipped the scorched paper back into her cloak.

"Then we write it back in."
