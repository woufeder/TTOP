# Star Charts, Crowns, and Atlas Scrolls

Every atlas scroll records three things at once: borders, taxation, and marching routes. That is why the map pages and the royal records should eventually reference each other.

## What the map pages should do

Map pages should do more than display an image. They should combine a regional overview, hotspot data, and next-step reading in one layout.

### Recommended fields

- mapImage: the main regional image
- hotspots: markers and lightbox content
- targetRegionSlug: a route from the world map into a regional map

## How the royal records can grow

The records page can stay table-based for now, then expand later with dynasty filters, sticky columns, and character cross-references.
